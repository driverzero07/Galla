## Full description (max 4000 characters)

**English**

Galla is a simple khata and billing app for small shops in Pakistan.

- Make a bill in 3 taps: open Bill, tap the item, tap "Bill banao".
- Keep every customer's udhaar and payments in one khata. See who owes what
  and send a WhatsApp reminder.
- Know your stock. Get a warning when an item is running low.
- Record purchases from suppliers and what you still owe them.
- Works fully without internet. Everything is saved on your phone first and
  is backed up automatically when the net comes back.
- Moving from the paper copy? Type the names and balances, or import a CSV /
  Excel file.
- Export all your data as CSV files any time.
- Lock with a 4-digit PIN. Roman Urdu throughout, big buttons, light on old
  phones.

**Roman Urdu**

Galla chhoti dukan ke liye aasan khata aur billing app hai.

- 3 tap mein bill: Bill kholo, saman par tap karo, "Bill banao" dabao.
- Har customer ka udhaar aur wasooli ek khata mein. Kis ne kitna dena hai
  dekho aur WhatsApp par yaad dehani bhejo.
- Saman ka stock dekho. Kam hone par khabar milti hai.
- Supplier se kharid aur un ka baqaya likho.
- Net ke baghair bhi chalta hai. Pehle phone mein save hota hai, net aate hi
  khud backup ho jata hai.
- Purani copy se data lana hai? Naam aur baqaya likho ya CSV / Excel file
  se lo.
- Apna sara data kisi bhi waqt CSV mein nikalo.
- 4 number ka PIN. Roman Urdu, bare buttons, purane phone par bhi halka.
